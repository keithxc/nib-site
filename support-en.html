Support

For problems or suggestions, open an issue on GitHub Issues. Issues are public and require a GitHub account; please do not attach real bills, payment screenshots or other personal information.

FAQ

  • Where is my ledger? See Settings → Data location. The ledger is one Markdown file per year and can be opened in the Files app.
  • How do I export? Use Settings → Export & backup.
  • How do I delete data? See “Export and deletion” in the privacy policy.

Category and note suggestions

Recognition, categorization, and note suggestions run on your phone. One pack supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English together, including mixed-language travel receipts, regardless of interface language, device region, or currency. You can correct suggestions; rule updates do not rewrite original receipt note text or existing entries.

Nib enables official-rule downloads by default; you can turn them off in Settings. When enabled, the next check is due 72 hours after a successful check; a failed check can retry after one hour while the app is in use. Entering the foreground or opening Settings can trigger a due check. Updates download static rule files from fixed GitHub Pages URLs. Nib does not attach receipts, ledgers, or user/device identifiers to requests; the hosting service may process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses. See the privacy policy. Offline, disabled downloads, or failed update validation leave validated local rules or the bundled fallback available.

Foreign-currency booking and exchange rates

Nib converts foreign-currency transactions at historical rates for the transaction date into the ledger’s base currency at booking, retaining the signed original amount and currency. Each transaction is counted once in statistics using its fixed booked amount; the original amount is not counted again. On non-publication days, the latest available prior rate may be used; its actual date is saved with the entry. New rates do not revalue recorded entries. After changing the base currency, past booked currencies remain intact and are shown separately.

Settings → Exchange rates → Download historical exchange rates is enabled by default, separately from recognition-rule downloads. Only when a foreign-currency booking needs a rate unavailable in the local cache does Nib request a complete historical rate table with a fixed USD base and a bounded date window from Frankfurter’s fixed public endpoint. The service receives the date window and ordinary metadata such as your IP address, but no entries, amounts, or currencies selected by you. Viewing recorded entries and base-currency bookings do not request rates. With downloads disabled or offline, you can use available cached rates or enter a manual rate for one transaction. See the privacy policy.
