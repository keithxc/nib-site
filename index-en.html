Nib

Screenshot expense tracker for iPhone

After paying, take a screenshot or photo. Nib recognizes the amount on your device, you pick a category, and the entry is saved. Your ledger is a Markdown file of your own, stored where you choose.

  • Text recognition runs entirely on the device
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking
  • One Markdown table per year, exportable at any time
