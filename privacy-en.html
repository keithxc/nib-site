Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-17 · Nib for iOS

Summary

Nib has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDK. Entries, images, text recognition, categorization, and note suggestions are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. Optional official-rule downloads request only fixed public static files; the hosting service may process the request metadata described below.

What the app processes on your device

  • Entries: amounts, categories, notes and dates are saved as Markdown ledger files on your device.
  • Images: screenshots and receipt photos that you capture, pick from Photos, paste or send through the system share sheet are read on the device with Apple’s built-in text recognition. Originals stay on the device and are deleted automatically 7 days after the entry is saved by default; you can change this in Settings. Images of unconfirmed items are kept.
  • Settings: preferences such as appearance, base currency and image retention are stored on the device.

Optional official-rule downloads

Compatible versions of Nib enable official-rule downloads by default; you can turn them off in Settings. When enabled, the next check is due 72 hours after a successful check; a failed check can retry after one hour while the app is in use. Entering the foreground or opening Settings can trigger a due check. It downloads a version manifest and the corresponding static JSON rules from fixed GitHub Pages URLs. All languages share one pack; interface language, device region, and currency do not select the download.

Nib does not attach receipts, images, recognized text, ledger content, usage records, or user/device identifiers added by Nib to these requests. GitHub Pages still receives ordinary network request metadata, such as IP address, request time, requested path, and standard request headers, subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement linked below. Downloaded rules are used only on your device for category and note suggestions; they do not rewrite original receipt note text or existing entries. If downloads are disabled, the device is offline, or update validation fails, the app continues using validated local rules or its bundled fallback.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to photograph a receipt; the photo is recognized on the device.
  • Photos: the system photo picker gives the app only the images you select; the app does not access the rest of your library.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap paste.

You can revoke camera access at any time in system Settings.

Storage location and iCloud

The ledger is stored where you choose: the Nib folder in iCloud Drive, on the device, or a folder you select. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, syncing is performed by that service according to your system settings and its privacy policy; the developer cannot access these files.

Share extension and Shortcuts

Text and images sent through the share sheet or Shortcuts are held in the app’s shared container on the device until you confirm them in the app. They do not leave the device.

Export and deletion

You can export all entries from within the app. Deleting entries, deleting ledger files or removing the app removes the corresponding data; ledger files kept in iCloud Drive or another folder must be deleted by you in the Files app.

Children

Nib is not directed at children. Hosting request metadata associated with official-rule downloads is described above.

This website

This website and the static official-rule files are hosted by GitHub Pages, without analytics scripts or third-party fonts. GitHub may process request information, such as your IP address, for website visits and rule downloads; the GitHub Privacy Statement applies.
